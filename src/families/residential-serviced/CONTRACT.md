# Residential serviced tower

Exports `family: BuildingFamily` from [index.ts](index.ts), using the shared [family API](../api.ts). This family owns its plan, visible concrete skin, graphite piers, spandrels, condensers and service risers. It imports no sibling family implementation.

`plan` accepts a finite CCW rectangle at least 16 m on both sides, at least three floor heights of 2.6 m or more, a seed and optional `fixedFaces`. Invalid dimensions, winding, skew, or floor heights raise `RangeError`. Preferred ground height is 3.6 m; supplied heights remain authoritative.

Free plans inset the parcel by 0.8 m (a minimum 14.4 m envelope). The front-right corner follows a 4 m radius with four straight facets. The other three corners remain square. Fixed plans preserve all four supplied corners and edge numbers exactly. All floors share the footprint; one group identifies each actual floor. Ground sections have empty window arrays and flat paired-glass entrance fields. Upper windows have a 1.12 m sill, 0.42 m head band and fitted two-row pane grids. Narrow windows continue around the four corner facets. Sections exactly partition every edge; no decorative balcony is marked as usable.

`decorate` emits complete wall skin, floor bands and corner returns around actual opening reservations. Hidden rear faces seated on the host shell or visible skin are omitted; all exterior faces and cut returns remain. It uses floor elevations directly, sets each part's owning floor and restores `builder.floor`. Windows, glazing housings, door frames, cassettes, clearance, pocket movement, transoms and supplied apertures are excluded, including envelopes extending across aligned floors. Rectangular reservation subtraction applies to every skin and band. Attachments are rejected as complete assemblies when they intersect a reservation.

Weathered concrete spandrels wrap the curved corner. Graphite piers mark the room module boundaries. Windows remain at their planned surface depths: 0.12 m in free plans and 0.62 m in fixed plans. Fixed outer bands are recessed by 0.20 m, making room for attached services inside the exact parcel. Visible skin returns are 0.16 m deep, independent of the host's 0.78 m structural backing and full-depth opening returns.

Alternate flat bays carry supported condensers: 1.08 m casings, projecting lids, circular fans, metal rings, bars, side vents, two L brackets and diagonal struts. All condenser geometry lies 0.15–0.98 m above the actual floor, below the window sill. Maximum free projection is 0.432 m; fixed fittings remain inside the parcel. Return lines join exposed supported risers on solid piers. Risers turn into explicit wall entries at their top and bottom, and before or after a blocked storey. A condenser is omitted if its riser cannot be fitted. No bridge, stair, clothes line or external connection is invented. The host's `fittings` simplification reduces repeated fan segments, grille bars, vents, collars and bracket struts while retaining recognizable casings and fitted L supports. The final `coverings` stage keeps complete service assemblies on every third floor starting at floor 1, and every fourth bay; all visible wall, band, window and frame geometry remains.

Materials bind final catalog slots: `concrete-monolith/mid#weathered`, `exterior-graphite-coating/mid#native`, `paired-frame-metal/mid#surface` and `service-alloy/poor#brushed`, all under `cyberpunk`. The service slot resolves through the catalog alias on `interior-alloy/poor`, retaining its `brushed` variant. All bound variants and referenced map files were verified. Weathering is texture detail on continuous slabs; the facade does not use a small-tile surface pattern.

One deterministic low service cable joins neighboring installed condensers when a clear same-floor span exists. Its actual casing-side endpoints carry fitted sockets; five tube segments of 8 mm radius follow 0.10 m sag below the window sills. The complete span reserves its own envelope, survives reduced service density, and costs 104 triangles including both anchors. A missing condenser or blocked span never produces a dangling wire. No span is required on a narrow facade without two fitted units.
